# Code of Conduct

SwiftWin follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful in issues, pull requests and discussions. Harassment, insults, personal
attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not accepted. Disagree about code, not people.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people
who keep breaking them.

**Report a problem** privately to swiftwin-support@googlegroups.com. Reports are kept confidential.
